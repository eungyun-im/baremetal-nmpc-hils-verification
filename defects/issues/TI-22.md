## Summary
The final round's verdict script reported the original (PC, Ipopt) controller's pose-to-command delay as 26.6 ms mean and 39.4 ms p99. The correct values are 28.7 ms and 46.0 ms.

## How it was found
An independent review of the final report compared it with the raw data and noticed that only 70 % of the original controller's cycles were in the delay sample, the same share as cycles with a solve under 20 ms.

## Cause
The script paired two log topics only when they were within 20 ms. The original controller publishes one before the solve and the other after it, so every cycle with a solve longer than 20 ms was dropped. The bridge used by the MCU and SILS configurations publishes both at the same time and was not affected.

## Fix
Pair each delay message of the original controller with the next cycle-time message. Patch: `verification/final_round/errata/req_verdict_e2e_pairing.patch`. The value is a report-only item (NFR-04), so no verdict changed, and the other 15 rows of the verdict table are byte-identical before and after.

## What else the review found
A statement built on the wrong value ("without the CAN and safety wait the delay equals the original") was withdrawn. A second review found that the original controller's "163 ms maximum cycle" was a pre-start cycle caught at the edge of the judged window; the in-drive maximum is 61.7 ms. All corrections are listed in `verification/final_round/errata/ERRATA.md` and summarized in `docs/final_round.md`.
